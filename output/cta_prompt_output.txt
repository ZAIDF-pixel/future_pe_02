Here are 10 catchy CTAs for a refreshments and ice cream shop, designed for Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts, targeting teenagers, students, and young adults.
1. Soft CTAs
Friendly and casual — no pressure.
1. “Craving something sweet? You know where to go!”
2. “Your next ice cream break is calling!”
3. “Go on, treat yourself to something chilled!”

2. Social CTAs
Encourage friends, comments, and sharing.
4. “Tag your ice cream buddy! 🍦”
5. “Who are you bringing for a chill treat?”
6. “Send this to someone who owes you ice cream!”
7. “Comment your all-time favorite ice cream flavor!”

3. Direct CTAs
Clear, action-oriented, and inviting.
8. “Drop by today and grab your favorite scoop!”
9. “Beat the heat — visit us for a refreshing treat!”
10. “Come try your new favorite flavor today!”
