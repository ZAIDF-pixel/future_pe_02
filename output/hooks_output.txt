Here are 4 attention-grabbing UGC ad hooks for the iPhone, designed for Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts. Each hook grabs attention within the first 3 seconds and targets fashionable people and working professionals.
1. Fashion & Lifestyle — “Your Outfit Needs This”




Hook 1
First 3 seconds: “Your outfit is fire… but your phone setup?”
Script: “Your outfit is fire… but your phone setup? The iPhone brings style, premium design, and a camera that makes capturing your everyday looks effortless. It's more than a phone — it's part of your vibe.”
CTA: “Upgrade your style. Explore the iPhone.”


2. Professional Lifestyle — “Still Wasting Time?”




Hook 2
First 3 seconds: “Busy all day? Your phone should keep up.”
Script: “Between meetings, emails, and deadlines, you need a phone that fits your pace. With the iPhone, you can stay connected, manage your day, and capture ideas on the go.”
CTA: “Make your workday smarter. Discover the iPhone.”


3. Camera & Social Media — “Wait Until You See This!”



Hook 3
First 3 seconds: “POV: Your phone just became your content partner.”
Script: “From outfit checks to weekend reels, the iPhone helps you capture the moments you want to share. Get creative, shoot your next look, and bring your content ideas to life.”
CTA: “Ready for your next post? Check out the iPhone.”


4. Premium & First Impressions — “Small Detail, Big Difference”



Hook 4
First 3 seconds: “Your everyday essentials say a lot about you.”
Script: “Your watch, your outfit, your workspace — every detail reflects your style. Add the iPhone's sleek design and powerful features to your everyday routine.”
CTA: “Make every detail count. Explore the iPhone.”



Quick filming tips to boost engagement
- 0–3 seconds: Deliver the hook directly to the camera with a strong expression.
- 3–7 seconds: Show the iPhone in action with quick, clean cuts.
- 7–12 seconds: Demonstrate the lifestyle benefit — fashion, productivity, or content creation.
- Final 2 seconds: Display the phone and deliver the CTA.
- Style: Use natural lighting, authentic reactions, trending audio, and on-screen captions.
My recommendation: Start with Hook 1 for a fashion-focused audience and Hook 2 for working professionals. Both connect the iPhone to a relatable everyday need rather than relying only on product features.
